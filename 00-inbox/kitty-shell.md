**Kitty Shell** is the command-line environment provided by the **Kitty** terminal emulator. It combines your preferred shell (such as Bash, Zsh, or Fish) with Kitty's advanced terminal capabilities, including GPU-accelerated rendering, tabs, split panes, image display, remote control, and customizable keyboard shortcuts.

It is commonly used by developers, system administrators, and DevOps engineers as a high-performance terminal for software development, Linux administration, container management, Kubernetes/OpenShift operations, and general command-line workflows.
